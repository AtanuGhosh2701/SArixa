Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF documents really safe? Do you upload them anywhere?

Yes, your files are 100% private! We never upload your PDFs to any server. Every action—whether you're rearranging pages, cropping, drawing, or adding text—happens locally inside your browser. Your documents never leave your device.

What can I do with the SArixa PDF Organizer?

You get a full visual workspace! You can drag and drop pages to reorder them, rotate upside-down pages, delete unwanted pages, insert blank pages or images between existing pages, crop and adjust margins, draw freehand, add custom text, and apply watermarks or page numbers.

Can I combine pages from multiple PDF files or extract specific pages?

Absolutely! After loading your first PDF, just click "Add another PDF" to bring in more documents and mix-and-match their pages. You can also select any single page or multiple pages and click "Extract" to save them separately as a PDF, PNG, or JPG.

Is this tool completely free? Will there be any watermarks?

It is 100% free with no hidden charges, no sign-ups, and zero forced watermarks. Only the custom watermarks that you choose to add yourself will appear on your exported PDF.

What if I make a mistake while editing or deleting a page?

No worries at all! You can use the Undo and Redo buttons on the toolbar to step back through your actions, or click the Reset button on any modified page to restore it to its original state.

Does the PDF Organizer work smoothly on mobile phones?

Yes! The workspace is built for touchscreens on Android phones, iPhones, and tablets. You can smoothly drag pages to reorder them, pinch or expand pages for a closer look, and even use the offset pointer for neat finger drawing.

What is a PDF Organizer?

A PDF organizer is an all-in-one visual tool that lets you view every page of your PDF as a thumbnail card. Instead of dealing with clunky software, you can reorder, rotate, delete, insert, or edit individual pages in seconds.

How to Organize PDF Pages?

  1. Select or drag and drop your PDF file into the box.
  2. Drag page cards to reorder them, or click a page to rotate, crop, draw, or add text.
  3. Click "Export Modified PDF" to build your file.
  4. Preview, rename, and download your organized PDF!
