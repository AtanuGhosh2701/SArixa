Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Open and Permissions password?

The Open Password is required just to view the document. The Permissions (Owner) Password is a master key used to restrict people from printing or copying the content, even if they can read it.

Is my document safe during encryption?

Absolutely. SArixa uses purely client-side JavaScript and WebAssembly (WASM). Your PDF never leaves your computer, making it 100% secure from data leaks.

What level of encryption does SArixa use?

We use AES-256 bit encryption. This is the highest and most robust security standard available for PDF documents, trusted by banks, military, and governments worldwide.

Can SArixa recover my password if I forget it?

No. Because we do not store your files or passwords on any server, a lost password cannot be recovered. Please memorize or securely save your passwords.

Will the password work on all devices and PDF viewers?

Yes! The AES-256 encryption applied by SArixa is universally supported. You can open your protected files seamlessly on Adobe Acrobat, Google Chrome, Mac Preview, iOS, and Android devices.

Can I remove the password later?

Yes. As long as you remember the original password, you can use any standard PDF software (like Adobe Acrobat) or our upcoming "Unlock PDF" tool to remove the security permanently.
